title=Dead Man's Map
description=[h1]Dead Man's Map[/h1]
description=A multiplayer mod for Project Zomboid Build 42 that turns a dying player's map annotations into a physical Annotated Map, dropped where they died.
description=
description=Any player who reaches the death site can pick up the map and open it. Its paper view loads the active world and is bounded around the copied markings, or around the death location when empty. 
description=
description=The Map item contains the annotations, but not the dead player's explored-map or fog-of-war data. Add the player's Annotated map to your own by right clicking the map & choosing Copy markings to my map
description=
description=[h2]When You Die[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Every text note and symbol on the personal map is copied onto a fresh map named "<Player Name>'s Annotated Map". Empty maps are also dropped
description=[*]The named Annotated Map is dropped on the death square, or the nearest usable square
description=[*]The dead character's personal map is always wiped of all annotations, giving them a fresh map with their new life
description=[/list]
