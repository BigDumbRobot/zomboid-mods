title=COMPUTER Safehouse and Land Claims
description=[h1]COMPUTER: Safehouse and Land Claims[/h1]
description=A Safehouse and Land Claims overhaul service module for the [b]Knox County Information and Property Network[/b].
description=
description=Claiming property is a multi-step process. Claims expire, forcing players to move around the map.
description=
description=[h2]Government Services Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Multi-Step Ownership Chain[/b] - Additional crafting, travel, and processing requirements make claiming buildings and land on multiplayer servers more of a challenge
description=[*][b]Government Service Zones[/b] - Adds a new KIPN World Registry zone type that admins can draw and manage with the Zone tool
description=[*][b]Government Services BBS[/b] - Computers inside Government Zones provide KIP Network access for converting Registration Forms into Safehouse Deeds and Excavation Permits
description=[*][b]Safehouse Claims Overhaul[/b] - Replaces vanilla safehouse claiming with KIPN’s token-based ownership system
description=[*][b]Excavation Land Claims[/b] - Allows players to claim open land using Excavation Permits for Small, Medium, or Large parcel claims. Sandbox-defined dimensions and prices for each size
description=[*][b]Property Records[/b] - Players can review and release their own safehouse and excavation claims from any computer
description=[*][b]Claim Overlays[/b] - KIPN claim boundaries can be viewed using a dedicated button in the vanilla safehouse window. Proximity overlays display proposed Excavation claim boundaries and the validity of those boundaries before confirming a claim. The overlay will remain visible to the player who claimed until toggled off (right click menu)
description=[*][b]Admin Safehouse Overlay[/b] - Admins can see all safehouses and land claims in their standard map. Right click with map open to toggle safehouse view
description=[*][b]Ownership Renewal[/b] - Ownership claims expire on an admin-configurable schedule and must be renewed to remain active
description=[*][b]Server Authoritative[/b] - Document issuance, token validation, ownership state, renewal, and claim enforcement occur on the server. Clients request and view
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Path to Ownership[/h2]
description=Each step advances the claim through a separate physical token state. Losing the required document breaks the chain for that player. Documents can be sold, lost, stolen, or expire. Claims are never final until a Deed or Permit is converted to owned property.
description=[olist]
description=[*][b]Materials[/b] - Gather the required materials to craft a Registration Form
description=[*][b]Registration Form[/b] - A physical world item crafted by the player and required to obtain a Safehouse Deed or Excavation Permit through KIPN Government Services
description=[*][b]Processing[/b] - Carry the Registration Form to a powered computer inside a Government Zone and process it using the KIP Network. The Deed and Permit crafting options remain locked until a valid Registration Form is in your inventory
description=[*][b]Claim Document[/b] - Select either a Safehouse Deed or Excavation Permit. Permits are available for purchase in Small, Medium, or Large sizes. The server issues the Deed or Permit and consumes the Registration Form
description=[*][b]Ownership Claim[/b] - Carry a Deed or Permit to an eligible building or parcel of land. Right click and choose claim. If your chosen location is not explicitly restricted by the admin, [b]any[/b] location is eligible for claim
description=[*][b]Renewal or Release[/b] - Review and release ownership through KIPN’s Property Records menu. Released or expired property immediately becomes available for a new player
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]Government Service Zones[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Government Zones are assigned with the KIPN Zone Admin tool
description=[*]Any powered computer inside a Government Zone gains the Government Services document-processing services
description=[*]Registration Forms can only be converted into Safehouse Deeds and Excavation Permits inside a Government Zone
description=[*]Property Records can be accessed from any KIP Network computer
description=[*]Computers can be installed, removed, or relocated without breaking zone functionality
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Safehouse Claims[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]KIPN overrides the vanilla safehouse-claiming process
description=[*]Any eligible building outside admin-assigned Do Not Claim Zones and protected KIPN Service Zones can be claimed
description=[*]Sandbox-assigned padding expands the protected boundary surrounding a claimed building
description=[*]Ownership, access, trespass, and respawn rules are configurable by the server administrator
description=[*]A KIPN Safehouse Zone button is added to the vanilla safehouse view
description=[*]The KIPN Safehouse Zone view displays recorded KIPN claim boundaries and can be toggled on or off
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Excavation Land Claims[/h2]
description=Excavation Permits allow players to claim open land that is not attached to an existing building.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Small, Medium, and Large[/b] Excavation Permit sizes
description=[*]Admin-defined dimensions for each permit size
description=[*]Individually configurable permit prices
description=[*]Proximity overlays display the complete proposed claim boundary before the permit is redeemed
description=[*]The proposed boundary remains visible until the proximity overlay is toggled off
description=[*]Claims are blocked inside Do Not Claim Zones and protected KIPN Service Zones
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Property Records and Renewal[/h2]
description=Property Records provide a centralized view of a player’s KIPN-managed property.
description=[list]
description=[*]Review owned safehouse and excavation claims from any computer
description=[*]Release claims directly through the KIP Network Terminal
description=[*]Players must renew ownership claims periodically
description=[*]Expired or released property immediately returns to the pool of claimable buildings and land
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Configuration[/h2]
description=All options are on the shared KIPN sandbox page.
description=[list]
description=[*]Safehouse claim padding
description=[*]Small, Medium, and Large Excavation Permit dimensions
description=[*]Individual Excavation Permit prices
description=[*]Safehouse and excavation ownership limits
description=[*]Ownership expiration and renewal schedules
description=[*]Safehouse access, trespass, and respawn rules
description=[*]Additional property restrictions and claim enforcement policies
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]Server-level configuration changes require a server restart.[/b]
