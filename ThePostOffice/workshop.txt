description=[h2]Postal Services[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Admin-spawned Small, Medium, and Large PO Boxes in Post Office zones, with sandbox-defined capacities
description=[*]Spawned PO Boxes purchasable by players for a sandbox-defined amount (vanilla money, 3rd party currency, or both)
description=[*]PO Box ownership and key access - only the purchasing player can access items
description=[*]Paid copying of existing PO Box keys; copied keys retain access to the original box
description=[*]PO Box fulfillment for marketplace purchases, event prizes, and supported 3rd party marketplace deliveries
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Marketplace & Economy Integration[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Built-in admin shop managed by server staff with configurable catalog and prices
description=[*]Player inventory currency included in the displayed marketplace balance
description=[*]Inventory-first payment handling with supported provider balances
description=[*]Normalized catalog, balance, and purchasing interfaces
description=[*]Adapter API for compatible third-party economy and marketplace providers
description=[*]Completed purchases delivered to escrow until retrieved from a PO Box
description=[/list]
