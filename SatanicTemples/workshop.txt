description=[h1]Satanic Temples: Skill Recovery Ritual[/h1]
description=A multiplayer mod that adds Skill Recovery Satanic Temple Zones to the Zone Editor and a server-authoritative ritual for partial to full skill XP recovery.
description=
description=After you die, lay your sacrifices on The Dark Lord's Dumpster Altar to initiate a skill recovery ritual. If you try to deceive, you will receive Satan's Wrath. The punishment is severe, including bodily harm, bleeding, and in some cases, death.
description=
description=[h2]Temple Sites[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Temple Sites can be indoor/outdoor
description=[*]Dumpster Altar - 2 tiles, either half accepts sacrifices - 1 tile proximity around the Altar to drop large animal sacrifices
description=[*]Campfire - required to be lit during Skill Recovery Ceremony
description=[*]Cult-styled mannequins
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Skill Recovery Ritual[/h2]
description=The skill recovery ritual is a multi-stage process at the Altar
description=[olist]
description=[*]Place your sacrificial items into or around the Dumpster Altar
description=[*]Light the campfire
description=[*]Click Make a Sacrifice (commitment phase)
description=
description=Sacrifices take time to connect with the underworld & complete the offering. Stray more than 2 tiles from the altar and the ritual breaks with nothing recovered. Skills are only redeemed upon successful ritual completion. You can not escape Satan's Wrath.
description=
description=[h3]Offering Analysis & Tiers[/h3]
description=The Dark Lord evaluates all items in both altar containers and selects the highest-value sacrifice
description=[list]
description=[h3]Tier 1: Player Corpse Sacrifice (Highest Value)[/h3]
description=[list]
description=[*]Corpses may be carried in both hands instead of dragging, allowing more practical transport to Temple Sites
description=[*]Another player's corpse is the most powerful sacrifice
description=[*]First 5 uses per victim = full recovery strength
description=[*]Subsequent uses per victim = skill recovery declines by 1% per repeat victim use for the remainder of the wipe
description=[*]Your own corpse and zombie corpses are rejected, leading to severe punishment
description=[*](Optional) Rewards can spawn beside the sacrificer (disabled by default)
description=[/list]
description=[h3]Tier 2: Rare Items (Mid Value)[/h3]
description=[list]
description=[*]Default rare items = [code]Base.Sledgehammer; Base.Sledgehammer2; Base.SledgehammerForged; Base.Katana; Base.AssaultRifle + 3×Base.556Clip[/code]
description=[*]Recovered skills scale with item quality:
description=[list]
description=[*]Perfect condition/full clip = full recovery strength
description=[*]Damage, breakage, missing ammunition = reduced skill recovery
description=[*]Broken items or empty magazines accepted but worth less
description=[*]Rare item sacrifice rituals can be repeated per-life until the player reaches full recovery
description=[*]Careful, don't be greedy - Attempts beyond fully recovered skills incur Satan's Wrath
description=[/list]
description=[*]Accepted items are defined in sandbox
description=[h3]Tier 3: Animal Sacrifice (Lowest Value)[/h3]
description=[list]
description=[*]Fully intact animal carcasses offer the lowest skill recovery
description=[*]Amount of recovered skills scales with animal size
description=[*]Butchered animal heads accepted at much lower value (~15% of full carcass)
description=[*]Multiple animal offerings accepted up to the rare-item ceiling each life
description=[*]Attempts beyond the cap incur minor wrath (3-9 damage)
description=[/list]
description=[/list]
description=[h2]Satan's Wrath[/h2]
description=The Dark Lord takes offence to insufficient sacrifices and greed. Every insufficient sacrifice is punished with wounds and health loss, even if the player runs away. In some cases, wrath is fatal
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Full Wrath[/b]: randomized wounds (1-2 deep wounds, 1-3 scratches) + major health damage
description=[*][b]Minor wrath[/b]: single scratch + 3-9 damage (applied to animal greed attempts)
description=[*][b]Attempted Self-corpse offering[/b]: severe punishment (80 damage + broken bones + wounds)
description=[/list]
description=[i]Wrath effects are server-authoritative and synchronize to the client's body damage system.[/i]
description=[/list]
description=[h2]Skill Recovery Sandbox Settings[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Skill recovery saves per-account, per-skill personal-best XP
description=[*]Recovery strength is a 0-100 share of missing raw cumulative XP, not a percentage of visible skill levels
description=[*]For scale: strength 20 can restore roughly level 6 from a previously level-10 standard skill
description=[/list]
description=[h2]Key Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]All zone mutations, overlap validation, fixture placement, and XP changes run on the server
description=[*]Temple items are protected from pickup, dismantling, and destruction
description=[*]Configurable sandbox settings for Satanic Temples, recovery rates, and failure penalties
description=[/list]
description=[h2]Configuration[/h2]
description=All gameplay settings under Sandbox → Satanic Temples. Item recipes use this grammar:
description=alternative;alternative | requiredPart+requiredPart | quantity*Module.Item
description=
description=Example: Base.Sledgehammer;Base.Katana;Base.AssaultRifle+3*Base.556Clip
description=
