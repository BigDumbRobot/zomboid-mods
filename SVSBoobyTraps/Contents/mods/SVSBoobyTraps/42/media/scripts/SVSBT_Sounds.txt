/************************************************************
 * Spy vs Spy: Booby Traps — Build 42 sounds
 *
 * Every clip here is played by the SERVER, through
 * Effects.PlayRandomSound: one of the family is picked server-side
 * so that every client in earshot hears the SAME clip for the same
 * event instead of each rolling its own, and each play also adds a
 * world sound, so nearby zombies come looking.
 *
 *  - SVSBT_Success1..5 -- a trap was successfully set, and a trap
 *    detonated. Detonations sound from the trap's own tile.
 *  - SVSBT_TrapSearch1..3 -- somebody is searching for traps.
 *  - SVSBT_TrapFound1..3 / SVSBT_TrapNotFound1..3 -- how that
 *    search ended.
 *
 * Sound names are registered globally by name (GameSounds
 * keys its table on the bare name, not module.name), so the
 * module wrapper below is structural only and the SVSBT_
 * prefix is what keeps these from colliding with vanilla or
 * other mods.
 ************************************************************/
