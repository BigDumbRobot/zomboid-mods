/************************************************************
 * Spy vs Spy: Booby Traps — MAD Magazine recipe series
 *
 * Most issues teach exactly one NeedToBeLearn craftRecipe; a few teach two
 * related ones. Reading is a permanent character unlock; the matching
 * skill, feature, and ingredient requirements still apply afterward.
 *
 * Cover-source mapping:

 *   No. 7 svsGen2.PNG     -> Rectal Reorganizer

 *   No. 10                -> Shotgun Door Trap
