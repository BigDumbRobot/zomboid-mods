description=Read MAD magazines, scavenge the parts for your trap, and wreak havoc. Careful though, you're not the only spy in Knox County.

description=[h2]Entry-Level Traps[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Water Bucket Trap[/b] - Soaks clothing and scratches their head.
description=[*][b]Nail Board[/b] - A board. With nails. Feet, footwear, tires, zombies: all can get some. 4 uses total. can be lethal
description=[*][b]Trip Wire[/b] - Cross the line, sound a house alarm & notify the trap setter.
description=[*][b]Bear Trap[/b] - Reusable, brutal, and just as likely to mangle the hand that sets it as the leg that finds it.
description=[*][b]Shotgun Trap[/b] - Set at door tiles. Cross the trap and get blasted.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Advanced Espionage[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Explosive Parcel[/b] - Hidden in a mailbox, trash can, or dumpster. Check them at your own risk.
description=[*][b]Biological Agent[/b] - sealed jar of an unknown substance. When opened and set, the trap releases poisonous gas for 72 hrs. Contact means immediate and lingering health loss
description=[*][b]Anthrax[/b] - Contaminate any literature document. Immediate + lingering health effects to the first person to read.
description=[*][b]Ignition Charge[/b] - Starting the car triggers an explosion. Engine damage, fire & player damage
description=[*][b]Rectal Reorganizer[/b] - Rigged projectile device. The next person to sit in the driver's seat will not be sitting comfortably.
description=[*][b]Vehicle Tamper Alarm[/b] - Covers every seat, door, and window. Notifies the trap owner when detonated
description=[*][b]Remote Immobilizer[/b] - sync a controller, then kill the engine from anywhere, anytime, with zero warning.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Counter-Espionage[/h2]
description=Every trap can be inspected, disarmed, reversed, or salvaged. If you have read the magazine, you can detect the trap. The rest you find the hard way.
