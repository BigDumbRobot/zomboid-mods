description=A service module for the [b]Knox County Information and Property Network[/b]. Vehicle Services replaces open-access vehicle use with a token-based, multi-step [b]vehicle ownership claim overhaul[/b]
description=
description=Claiming a vehicle is a chain of events and each step is a risk.
description=
description=[h2]Vehicle Services Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Multi-Step Ownership Chain[/b] - Additional crafting, travel, and processing requirements make claiming vehicles on multiplayer servers more of a challenge
description=[*][b]Physical Bearer Tokens[/b] - Registration Forms and Vehicle Titles are prerequisites to ownership. They are world items which need to be crafted. They can be traded, sold, looted, dropped, destroyed, or left to expire
description=[*][b]Dealership Zones[/b] - The KIPN World Registry will recognize any admin-drawn Dealership Zone. Vehicle ownership claims are allowed within Dealership zones only
description=[*][b]Trespass Rules[/b] - Sandbox-assigned non-owner access enforcement covering seating, mechanics, towing, doors, and windows
description=[*][b]Vehicle Records[/b] - Review and release held claims from any computer
description=[*][b]Server Authoritative[/b] - Token issuance, validation, ownership state, and enforcement occur on the server. Clients request and view
description=[/list]
description=[h2]Path to Ownership[/h2]
description=Each step is a separate token state
description=[olist]
description=[*][b]Materials[/b] - Gather required materials to craft a Vehicle Registration Form
description=[*][b]Registration Form[/b] - A physical world item required to craft a Vehicle Title via KIPN's Vehicle Services. Registration forms expire if not converted to Vehicle Titles
description=[*][b]Processing[/b] - Carry the Form to a powered computer inside a Dealership zone and process it using the KIP Network. The Vehicle Title craft option remains locked until a valid Registration Form is in your inventory
description=[*][b]Vehicle Title[/b] - The server issues the Title. Vehicle Titles cannot be crafted directly. They will expire in 14 days if not used to make an ownership claim
description=[*][b]Ownership Claim [/b] - Carry the Title to a vehicle inside a Dealership Zone. Right click the car to claim. The server revalidates the token, the zone, and the vehicle, then consumes the Title
description=[*][b]Release[/b] - Ownership can be released by right clicking the vehicle or from KIPN's Vehicle Records menu. The vehicle releases immediately and is available to anyone with a valid Vehicle Title
description=[/olist]
description=[h2]Dealership Zones[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Dealership Zones are assigned with the admin Zone Editor tool
description=[*]Any powered computer inside a Dealership zone gains the Vehicle Registration service allowing players to craft Vehicle Titles
description=[*]Vehicles must be inside a Dealership Zone to make a successful claim
description=[*]Computers can be moved without breaking zone functionality
description=[/list]
description=[h2]Non-Owner Access Enforcement[/h2]
description=Trespass rules apply to every live vehicle claim
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Seating[/b] - A non-owner in a claimed vehicle is ejected and set down outside on the next movement tick or audit pass
description=[*][b]Gated By Sandbox[/b] - Mechanics, towing, and opening unlocked doors are each controlled by an individual sandbox toggle
description=[*][b]Always Denied[/b] - non-owner lock and unlock, window control, and smashing windows always denied regardless of settings
description=[*][b]Master Denial[/b] - A single no-access switch overrides every individual toggle
description=[*][b]Exempt[/b] - Owners, authorized players, and admins are unaffected
description=[/list]
description=[h2]Configuration[/h2]
description=All options are on the shared KIPN sandbox page.
description=[list]
description=[*]Independent toggles for Registration Form crafting and Title redemption
description=[*]Maximum claims per player
description=[*]Separate expiry windows for Registration Forms, Vehicle Titles, and ownership itself
description=[*]Processing time at the Dealership computer
description=[*]Per-interaction non-owner access toggles and the master denial switch
description=[/list]
