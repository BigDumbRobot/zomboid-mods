description=[h2]Walk with the Herd[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Camouflage[/b] - blood & guts on your body and clothing + crafted Face Masks and Skin Suits
description=[*][b]Stealth[/b] - sneaking keeps you hidden. Standing upright, walking, running, fighting and especially gunfire all give you away.
description=[*][b]The Whisper Meter[/b] - A meter to show how conspicuous you are. It appears when you apply any camouflage. Nearby zombies start to notice as the meter rises. At full, every zombie around you notices. Go quiet and they slowly lose interest, until they forget you entirely at zero.
description=[/list]
description=[h2]Wear the Dead[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Guts[/b] - butcher corpses for their guts and smear them on. They are heavy and rotting. Smearing guts makes you slow and sick. Guts deteriorate over 72 hrs.
description=[*][b]Chop Off Heads[/b] - Use zombie and player heads for further crafting. A sword or katana takes a single chop. A machete or axe takes a few chops. A short blade takes a long time and leaves you exhausted.
description=[*][b]Face Masks[/b] - cut the face from a head and sew it into a crafted mask. Face Masks are 3d objects and show a stretched,mutilated version of the face they were cut from. Your stealth counts 5x while wearing the Face Mask, you settle into stealth much faster, and zombies need more to notice you. Everything past arm's reach goes blurry through the eye holes. Masks deteriorate with use.
description=[*][b]Skin Suits[/b] - Flay a whole corpse into your very own crafted Skin Suit. Your stealth counts 10x while wearing the suit, stealth is almost instant, and zombies give you far more leeway. Suits deteriorate with time and wear. Treat them as you would leather & they last longer. A hard hit can tear them apart. In a suit, mask and 100% guts, only something drastic gives you away.
description=[*][b]Head Stakes[/b] - Crafting takes 1 head + 1 long stick. Right-click and Place it like furniture. Each head stake is a 3d object. A player's head keeps their own face and hair, zombie heads get a random living or rotting face, and faceless heads are flayed. Hats and masks can go on top for decoration. People and cars can't pass through them. A beautiful addition to your base.
description=[/list]
description=[h2]Your OPs are Trophies[/h2]
description=Player corpses give up [i]named[/i] heads, faces, masks and suits and retain the facial features. Wear your friends and enemies or decorate your base with their head on a stick!
description=[h2]The price you pay[/h2]
description=Wearing the dead is miserable. While any blood & guts remain on your skin, clothes, or weapons, you will be deeply unhappy, stressed and uncomfortable. Your character can develop corpse sickness which in extreme cases will cause death. Wash it all off to feel human again.
